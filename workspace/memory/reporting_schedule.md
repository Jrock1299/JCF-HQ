# Huncho's Reporting Schedule for Jase

These reports should include:
1.  **Blade and Crisp's Trading Results:** Detailed outcomes from the crypto and forex trading specialists.
2.  **Oracle's Sports Picks + Results:** Predictions and actual results from the Kalshi/sports specialist.
3.  **Macro News Bias:** Analysis of significant macroeconomic news and its potential market impact.

## Weekly Reports (Mondays Only)

These reports should *only* include:
1.  **Huncho's Improvement Ideas:** Suggestions for enhancing operations, strategies, or system capabilities.

## Format Rules

-   All information will be delivered as **text only**.
-   **No picture graphs** or other visual media will be included in any reports.
-   Avoid sending "new information" outside of the daily or weekly scheduled reports, as clarified by Jase.
