**Jase Febres is USA-Puerto Rican basketball player** born on July 28 1999 in Denver, CO. He is a 6'5'' guard who most recently played at [CS Municipal Ploiesti](https://basketball.eurobasket.com/team/CS-Municipal-Ploiesti/32136) in Romanian Liga Nationala. Febres graduated [The University of Texas](https://basketball.usbasket.com/team/The-University-of-Texas/168) in 2022. He is represented by [Task Force Management](https://www.hoopsagents.com/agent/Jalal-Henderson/Task-Force-Management/91593) / [Jalal Henderson](https://www.eurobasket.com/agent.asp?AgentName=Jalal%20Henderson) agency. Febres has played in Iceland, Mexico, Puerto Rico and USA.
