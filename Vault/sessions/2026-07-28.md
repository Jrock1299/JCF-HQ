# 2026-07-28 — Ruin fix, walk-forward, honest verdict on turtle_soup

Follow-up to the 2026-07-27 gate change. Jase ruled on the compounding
artefact previously flagged as "reported but not applied": fix the ruin-
input math the same way the drawdown-input math was fixed (real risk basis,
not compounded), re-run the walk-forward + sensitivity + baseline that
were scoped but not executed yesterday, and give an honest verdict — deploy
turtle_soup or not, and if yes, is it real ICT or mean reversion?

## 1. Ruin-input compounding artefact — fixed

**Symptom:** turtle_soup's MC ruin was 68-74 % on SPX / DE30 / XAU (13 %
on CHF/JPY), a figure that gated it out of PASS despite passing every
other criterion cleanly.

**Root cause (confirmed).** `monte_carlo_simulation` bootstrapped the raw
`pnl` series against a fixed $10 k starting balance. The pnl series was
compounded — trades sized off the growing balance, peaks of $7-53 M in the
backtest — so resampling a $-M-scale loss against a $10 k account wiped it
by construction. Same class of bug as the fake 468 % drawdown fixed
2026-07-25; that anchor was the drawdown DENOMINATOR, this is the ruin
INPUT.

**Fix.** MC ruin now bootstraps `r_multiple × FIXED_RISK_PER_TRADE`
(0.75 % × $10 k = $75), position-size-invariant. The old compounded MC is
retained on every row as `monte_carlo_compounded` so before/after is
visible in one file; `mc_basis` field records which series was used.

**Every other gate criterion unchanged.** Not a threshold move, a
correctness fix on the input feeding the existing 10 % ruin ceiling.

## 2. Walk-forward on turtle_soup — clean OOS on every window

7 / 7 OOS windows meet PF ≥ 1.2 AND Sharpe_ann ≥ 0.5 on all 4 instruments.
OOS numbers as strong as or stronger than IS on most windows. No regime
dependence, no fitting artefact.

## 3. Parameter sensitivity — mostly robust, one weak spot on SPX

Robust on DE30 / XAU / CHF_JPY across every knob variant. SPX has one
variant that collapses to PF 0.58 — real fragility on that instrument

## 4. Mean-reversion baseline — the key finding

Baseline (strip H4 bias + killzones, fade 24/7) OUT-PERFORMS turtle_soup
on 3/4 instruments (SPX/DE30/XAU) — higher PF, higher Sharpe, 4-5× the
trade count, double R-per-trade. Only CHF/JPY genuinely needs the ICT
filters. **The mechanism is post-sweep mean reversion, not ICT.**

**Honest interpretation.**
- **On the validated M15 basis:** all three MARGINAL. Sh_ann 2.4-3.1
  and MaxDD < 10% say the edge is visible; PF 1.22-1.28 sits below the
  1.5 PASS floor. 60d/71d of history is too short to clear the floor
  confidently — turtle_soup on OANDA needed 8 years (~190k bars) to
  produce PF 1.5+.
- **On H1 (2yr):** all three PASS the same gate. But this is a different
  timeframe than the strategy was validated on — H1 sweep dynamics are
  not M15 sweep dynamics. Strong evidence the mechanism transfers, NOT
  the same-basis validation Jase asked for.

**No Pine indicators generated.** Per the rule: "do NOT generate an
indicator for an instrument that failed validation." MARGINAL is not
PASS at same-basis; the H1 PASS is at wrong basis. Do not put an
un-validated signal in front of Jase's live eval.

**Path forward.** To close the same-basis validation, we need M15
futures history beyond 60d. Databento personal ~$100/mo, Polygon.io
similar, or Rithmic (which Lucid provides once the API integration
