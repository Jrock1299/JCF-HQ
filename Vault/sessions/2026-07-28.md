# 2026-07-28 — Ruin fix, walk-forward, honest verdict on turtle_soup

Follow-up to the 2026-07-27 gate change. Jase ruled on the compounding
artefact previously flagged as "reported but not applied": fix the ruin-
input math the same way the drawdown-input math was fixed (real risk basis,
not compounded), re-run the walk-forward + sensitivity + baseline that
were scoped but not executed yesterday, and give an honest verdict — deploy
turtle_soup or not, and if yes, is it real ICT or mean reversion?

## 1. Ruin-input compounding artefact — fixed

**Symptom:** turtle_soup's MC ruin was 68-74 % on SPX / DE30 / XAU (13 %
on CHF/JPY), a figure that gated it out of PASS despite passing every
other criterion cleanly.

**Root cause (confirmed).** `monte_carlo_simulation` bootstrapped the raw
`pnl` series against a fixed $10 k starting balance. The pnl series was
compounded — trades sized off the growing balance, peaks of $7-53 M in the
backtest — so resampling a $-M-scale loss against a $10 k account wiped it
by construction. Same class of bug as the fake 468 % drawdown fixed
2026-07-25; that anchor was the drawdown DENOMINATOR, this is the ruin
INPUT.

**Fix.** MC ruin now bootstraps `r_multiple × FIXED_RISK_PER_TRADE`
(0.75 % × $10 k = $75), position-size-invariant. The old compounded MC is
retained on every row as `monte_carlo_compounded` so before/after is
visible in one file; `mc_basis` field records which series was used.

**Every other gate criterion unchanged.** Not a threshold move, a
correctness fix on the input feeding the existing 10 % ruin ceiling.

**Before / after (turtle_soup):**

| Instrument | Ruin (compounded, old) | Ruin (fixed-size, new) | verdict shift |
|---|---:|---:|---|
| SPX500_USD | 74.10 % | **0.00 %** | FAIL → PASS |
| DE30_EUR   | 72.08 % | **0.00 %** | FAIL → PASS |
| XAU_USD    | 68.05 % | **0.00 %** | FAIL → PASS |
| CHF_JPY    | 13.40 % | **0.00 %** | FAIL → MARGINAL (PF 1.451 < 1.5) |

CHF/JPY still not PASS: the Sharpe / ruin / drawdown / regime criteria all
clear, but PF sits at 1.451, just under the 1.5 PASS floor. That's a real
finding, not the artefact. **Kept as MARGINAL; not promoted to the library.**

The rest of the 28-strategy matrix moves only where it should: ifvg/CHF_JPY
correctly grades ruin **99.43 %** now (was 78.7 % under the compounded
input, because that instrument's real numbers were losing on both bases —
PF 0.67, MaxDD 61.5 %, Sh_ann -1.56 — and the fixed-size ruin caught it
properly rather than being coincidentally similar).

## 2. Walk-forward on turtle_soup — clean OOS on every window

Rolling 2-year in-sample / 1-year out-of-sample, no parameter fitting
(turtle_soup has no free knobs the detector reads that aren't in
`PARAMS`, and PARAMS is not fit per-window — the honest walk-forward is
"does the aggregate IS number show up on the untouched OOS year?").

**7 / 7 OOS windows meet gate criteria (PF ≥ 1.2 AND Sharpe_ann ≥ 0.5)
on all 4 instruments.**

Representative windows (SPX / DE30 for brevity):

| Instrument | IS years | IS PF | IS Sh_ann | OOS year | OOS PF | OOS Sh_ann |
|---|---|---:|---:|---|---:|---:|
| SPX500_USD | 2018-19 | 1.465 | 3.576 | 2020 | 1.635 | 4.387 |
| SPX500_USD | 2023-24 | 1.519 | 3.587 | 2025 | 1.739 | 5.460 |
| DE30_EUR   | 2018-19 | 1.596 | 4.233 | 2020 | 1.766 | 5.155 |
| DE30_EUR   | 2022-23 | 2.182 | 6.173 | 2024 | 2.363 | 7.472 |

**OOS numbers are as strong as or stronger than IS on most windows.** No
window collapses, no regime dependence, no fitting artefact. The edge is
stable across 2018-19 chop, 2020 COVID vol, 2021-22 trending, 2023-24
range — all of it.

Flagged, not fatal to deployment at defaults; documented.

## 4. Mean-reversion baseline — the key question

Baseline strips the two remaining "ICT" pieces (H4 EMA-50 bias filter,
London/NY killzone) and fades the sweep 24/7 with no ICT confirmation at
all. Same sweep detection, same stop and target rules, same risk sizing.

Result — 4 of 4 instruments show the two are DIFFERENT, but **the direction
is not what the label suggested.** On 3 of 4 instruments (SPX / DE30 / XAU)
the plain baseline **out-performs** turtle_soup:

| Instrument | Turtle n | Turtle PF | Turtle Sh_ann | Turtle R | Baseline n | Baseline PF | Baseline Sh_ann | Baseline R |
|---|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| SPX500_USD | 4,483 | 1.552 | 2.369 | 0.27 | 19,074 | **2.533** | **3.746** | **0.50** |
| DE30_EUR   | 3,282 | 1.893 | 2.780 | 0.38 | 17,555 | **2.438** | 2.815 | **0.50** |
| XAU_USD    | 3,678 | 1.584 | 2.417 | 0.25 | 19,071 | **2.240** | **3.229** | **0.41** |
| CHF_JPY    | 3,232 | **1.451** | **2.552** | **0.19** | 18,698 | 1.355 | 1.240 | 0.18 |

Read the SPX row plainly: the plain 24/7 sweep-fade takes 4.3× as many
trades and produces a higher profit factor, higher Sharpe, and double the
expectancy-per-trade. **The "ICT filters" (killzone + H4 bias) are
filtering out winning trades**, not adding edge. Same story on DE30 and
XAU.

Only on CHF/JPY does the ICT wrapper measurably help. There the killzone +
H4 bias more than double the annualised Sharpe (2.55 vs 1.24).

**The mechanism is post-sweep mean reversion, not ICT structure**, on 3 of
the 4 instruments turtle_soup PASSES. Say it plainly. The strategy is
deployable, but calling it "ICT" on those instruments is dressing it up.

## 6. Deployable — yes, with the caveat above

**Deploy? YES** — the strategy passes walk-forward on every OOS window
across every instrument, passes ruin under corrected math, and is
parameter-robust on 3 of the 4 instruments where it PASSES the gate.

**What is it? MEAN REVERSION** — not ICT structure, on the instruments it
passes on. The ICT-inspired trappings (killzones, H4 bias) do the strategy
active harm on SPX/DE30/XAU. Only CHF/JPY genuinely needs them, and CHF/JPY
does not PASS the current gate.

**Follow-on work worth doing (not now):**
1. Validate the plain 24/7 sweep-fade as its own strategy through the full
   gate. Higher trade count + higher PF + higher Sharpe on 3 of 4
   instruments — it's plausibly a better product than turtle_soup with
   less code. Would be a separate library entry with its own mechanism
   label ("post-sweep mean reversion, no filters").
2. Investigate the SPX parameter fragility (one variant PF 0.58). Which
   knob breaks it, and does the default hold with a fresh 6-month OOS?
3. CHF/JPY PF 1.45 is close enough to the 1.5 floor that a small
   improvement in the sweep detector might promote it. Not a threshold
   move — better selection.

**Not deployed live.** Blade / Edge / Jerry / Chad cron untouched. The
library is the record; live deployment is a separate PR after Jase's
explicit go-ahead.
